About the app

Lincoln Clinical Skills helps Lincoln Medical School students rehearse clinical skills on their own or with a peer. Choose an examination, procedure or history guide, start the timer, tick off each step as you go, then save the attempt and review what you missed. You can filter skills by Year 1 and Year 2.

The checklists come from the Lincoln Medical School Clinical Examination Handbook, Procedural Skills Handbook and Clinical History Taking Workbook.

The app is for self-directed practice. It is not a substitute for supervised teaching, and it is not a clinical decision tool.

Common questions

Where are my practice sessions stored?

Only on your phone (and in your own phone backups, if you use them). There is no account and nothing is sent to us. Deleting the app deletes your saved sessions.

How do I delete a saved session?

Open the History screen, choose the session, and tap the bin icon in the top corner.

A checklist looks wrong or out of date

Please let us know which skill and which step, using the contact details below. The handbooks are the authoritative source.
