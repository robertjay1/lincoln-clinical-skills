Lincoln Clinical Skills

Privacy policy

Last updated 16 October 2026

The short version

Lincoln Clinical Skills does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your device

When you save a practice attempt, the app stores it on your phone: which skill you practised, which steps you ticked, how long it took, and any reflection note you typed. Your Year 1 / Year 2 preference is stored the same way. It is not sent to us or to the University, and we cannot see it. Like other app data, it may be included in your own phone backups (iCloud, Google or computer) if you use them. It is deleted when you delete the app. You can also delete individual sessions in the app.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not send your practice data, device identifiers or usage information to us or to anyone else.

Demonstration videos

Some skills may offer an optional demonstration video. Videos only load if you choose to play one. They are streamed from a video host, which, like any website, receives standard technical information such as your IP address in order to deliver the video. No information about you or your practice is sent with the request.

Apple and Google

If you install the app through the App Store, TestFlight or Google Play, Apple or Google may collect information under their own privacy policies, for example crash reports and feedback you choose to send through their testing apps. We only see what they share with developers under their terms.

Children

The app is intended for medical students and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
